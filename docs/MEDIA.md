# School photography

Added on 26 October 2026 at the user's request, using photographs publicly shared by the school's Facebook account (sanjeevani.betul.1). The photographs show Garba celebrations on campus. Event dates are deliberately omitted because they were not independently confirmed.

Source permalinks, bilingual alt text and captions are maintained in `lib/media.js`. Images are stored locally in `public/images/school-life` as compressed WebP files, without embedded metadata or dependencies on Facebook CDN URLs. No Facebook or Instagram tracking embeds are loaded.

When the client provides original photos, replace the local assets and update the captions, alt text and source records in `lib/media.js`. `school.campusImage` in `lib/content.js` selects the homepage photograph. Do not use event imagery to imply unverified facilities or awards.
